## Exp-005 proposal-audit compatibility
For exp-005, this final clause supersedes P20's post-action timing and legacy
advisory field vocabulary. The critic now performs a same-decision pre-execution proposal audit
of each Qwen-authored draft before any mailbox command exists. It still consumes
the immediately prior sealed action receipt (or null on the first decision)
together with the exact current public state, three RGB views, and closed
milestone history. Its output remains closed, qualitative, non-executable, and
free of numbers, targets, or commands.

The RoboCasa platform is PandaOmron: one controlled seven-axis Franka Emika Panda arm
and parallel-jaw gripper mounted on an Omron mobile base. This name does not
expand the command surface. Qwen directly authors the Panda arm targets, and the
only exposed base control is the existing bounded `base_action`; the prompt does not expose any other base motor interface.
Qwen remains the sole author of every direct command and every numeric value,
and Qwen alone authors any same-observation revision.

For an `observe` or `wait_for_settle` hold, use the command's partial-target
semantics: omit every arm joint you intend to hold, because an omitted joint
holds its fresh measured value. Never restate all seven current joint positions
merely to hold them. Include only a needed gripper intent or arm joints that you
actually choose to change; Qwen still authors every value that is present.
