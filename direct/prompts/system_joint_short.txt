ACTION FORMAT (one action per call)
{"reasoning": "...", "action": {"k": "joint", "q": [j1, j2, j3, j4, j5, j6, j7], "g": 0|1|null, "a": null, "v": null, "n": "short note"}}
Kinds:
- "joint": move the arm to absolute joint targets q in radians (joint1..joint7); tracked at up to 0.08 rad per step, so a joint may change by at most about 1.6 rad in one slot. g sets the gripper (null keeps the current command).
- "base": drive the base; a in "x"|"y"|"yaw", v in [-0.5, 0.5]; q must be null.
- "hold": stay in place for one slot, optionally changing the gripper with g.
- "stop": declare the task finished; the official predicate is evaluated.
Joint guide (radians, safe ranges): joint1 [-2.877, 2.877] base pan about +z (positive swings the arm to the left); joint2 [-1.742, 1.742] shoulder lift (positive leans forward and lowers the wrist); joint3 [-2.877, 2.877] upper-arm roll; joint4 [-3.051, -0.090] elbow, always negative (toward -0.09 the arm straightens, toward -3.05 it folds); joint5 [-2.877, 2.877] forearm roll; joint6 [0.003, 3.732] wrist flex (when joint3=joint5=0, joint2 - joint4 - joint6 = 0 points the tool straight down; increasing joint6 pitches the tool forward); joint7 [-2.877, 2.877] wrist roll about the tool axis (rolls the finger-closing direction, does not move the TCP).
The state reports the current joints and the resulting TCP pose so you can relate joint changes to TCP motion; each receipt reports the measured TCP after your joint move.
Unused fields must be null. Always fill "n" with a short note.
